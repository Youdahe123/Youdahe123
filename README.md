### A Little About Me

Hey, I'm Youdahe!

I'm passionate about building technically challenging systems at the intersection of artificial intelligence, distributed computing, and high-performance infrastructure.

I spend a lot of my time exploring how to make AI systems more efficient, reliable, and scalable, from optimizing inference pipelines to designing distributed architectures capable of handling complex computational workloads.

I've also worked with startups on some pretty interesting AI engineering challenges.

### AI Engineering & Startup Work

I help startups tackle complex problems across the modern AI stack, including:

- LLM Inference Optimization: Designing high-throughput, low-latency inference pipelines with continuous batching, KV-cache management, quantization, and efficient GPU utilization. Exploring distributed inference strategies involving tensor parallelism, pipeline parallelism, and optimized model serving.

- Agentic AI & Evaluation Infrastructure: Building end-to-end evaluation frameworks for autonomous agents, including multi-step trajectory evaluation, tool-call verification, model-graded assessments, regression testing, and automated failure analysis across complex agent workflows.

- Reinforcement Learning & Model Alignment: Working with reinforcement learning pipelines, reward modeling, preference optimization, and post-training techniques such as PPO, DPO, and GRPO. Interested in scalable rollout generation, distributed training, and improving reasoning capabilities through feedback-driven optimization.

- Distributed Systems & Large-Scale Computing: Exploring fault-tolerant architectures, distributed task scheduling, asynchronous communication, concurrency control, and performance optimization across compute-intensive workloads.

- AI Infrastructure & MLOps: Designing scalable model-serving infrastructure, containerized deployment pipelines, Kubernetes-based orchestration, and cloud-native systems for training, evaluating, and deploying machine learning models.

I particularly enjoy problems where machine learning research meets systems engineering, especially when improving performance requires understanding everything from GPU memory constraints to distributed execution.
